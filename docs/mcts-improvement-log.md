## Evaluation and promotion rules

Each behavior change is compared with both the original frozen MCTS and the previous accepted incumbent, holding other behavior, engine and priors fixed. The first stage can share a comparison because both anchors initially coincide. Source used by child processes must be identified, import boundaries isolated, and running evaluation source kept immutable. A new incumbent is frozen only after its measured decision is recorded. Failed experiments retain their artifacts and remain off in later stages.

The running Stage 1/2 screens swap simulator sides within each ordered team pair and balance each team's aggregate exposure, but do not also reverse bot/team assignments within every exact matchup. Team-assignment interactions can therefore confound a within-matchup effect; these original schedules remain unchanged and their limitation is retained. Future screens use 15 four-game blocks (both simulator sides and both bot/team assignments), giving each of ten teams six games in each bot role, three per simulator side. Conditional 200-game confirmation v2 plans use the same stronger blocking and give each team 20 games in each bot role. Unrun v1 confirmation manifests are preserved as superseded. No confirmation result exists. These conditional confirmation manifests were frozen after some initial screening outcomes were available; no numerical promising threshold is claimed to have been preregistered before those outcomes.

Correctness fixtures reproduce a concrete defect before the fix. Relevant tests and small live smoke gates precede game screening. Each distinct comparison starts with at least 60 balanced games over at least ten fresh reserved teams, with sides swapped within blocks and styles represented. Promising variants expand to 200 or more games on fresh confirmation matchups. Counts alone do not establish significance. Seeds, teams, opponent versions, settings, hashes and stopping rules are frozen before outcomes; policy RNG seeds and simulator seeds are recorded separately. Old pilot teams cannot be relabelled as fresh. Public samples may overlap aggregate Smogon prior inputs; the actual reservation and leakage limits must be stated.

Reports retain wins, losses, draws and voids; side/team/style breakdowns; Wilson 95% intervals and team-cluster uncertainty; targeted mechanism metrics; preparation and actual native/search/CPU work; requested/completed/usable/failed worlds; fallbacks, rejected choices and recovery/restart reasons; p50/p95/p99/max decision latency. Turns are not independent games, and few team clusters limit uncertainty estimates. Unmatched external-opponent compute must be labelled.

Legality, successful-world retention and parent deadline isolation are promotion gates. Stage 4 holds total requested native effort near 1,200 ms while separately reporting actual work and restart cost; it does not change pooling or enable parallel worlds. Root support is not a calibrated probability. Stage 5 requires opportunities/exposure and wins as controls, followed by failure reproduction and counterexamples; signature correlation alone does not justify a guard. Stage 6 retains deterministic Tera and legal/guard filtering initially, and does not claim equilibrium play or deterministic native search.

Permitted conclusions are **correctness improved; strength unknown**, **promising but inconclusive**, **strength improvement supported on this evaluation**, **no detected benefit**, or **regressed**. Correct state reconstruction can be retained without claiming a win-rate gain. An inconclusive policy heuristic stays experimental/off. Resource- or provenance-limited work is reported with exact completed counts and remaining work.

## Stage 1: retain supported state across requests and switches

**Problem.** The baseline had exact active-request PP but rebuilt benched moves with 16 PP. A Kingambit that used all its Kowtow Cleave PP could therefore regain that move in the reconstructed search state after switching. Request reordering could also separate remembered information from its Pokémon. A fainted request's `0 fnt` condition no longer supplied max HP, and the adapter used 100 rather than a previously observed exact value. Successful Protect streaks were missing. A still-visible extended screen, weather or terrain could reach zero remaining turns merely because its normal duration had elapsed.

**Change.** `bot/parser.py` retains own request-derived move PP and max HP by stable Pokémon identity, and `bot/player.py` supplies requests to it. `decision/state.py` uses that memory for bench/fainted reconstruction; a new exact active request takes precedence. Publicly observed move use can estimate PP spend, including a revealed Pressure ability; called and locked repeat moves do not spend it again. Leppa restoration invalidates the remembered amount until an authoritative request refreshes it. These estimates are explicitly distinct from exact request PP.

The parser tracks observed successful Protect streaks and resets them after failure, switching or an intervening non-Protect turn. `decision/adapter.py` supplies the native Protect counter. A visible screen/weather/terrain beyond its normal duration is retained for a conservative one turn instead of being silently removed; the hidden true remaining duration is still uncertain. Optional contract fields and adapter diagnostics identify remembered/estimated state and partial-turn completion/source metadata. The latter supports later inference work but does not reveal an opponent's still-queued hidden move.

## Stage 2: separate revelations from uncertain inference

**Problem and reproduction.** Four focused probes ran in a separate process against the original frozen baseline: a called second move incorrectly excluded Choice sets; absence of Leftovers healing during an incomplete turn excluded the item; removed Stealth Rock was treated as evidence for Boots; and a first item revelation retained an incompatible older move period, causing needless inferred-rule recovery. All four baseline probes reproduced their mismatch. The candidate's `legacy` mode reproduced the same four; `reliable` mode reproduced none. Raw probes are preserved as `runs/mcts-improvements/stage2-{baseline,legacy,reliable}-reproduction.json`, with reproduction script `stage2-reproduce.py` in that directory. These are targeted correctness examples, not a claim that all inference problems are solved.

**Change.** `decision/belief.py` provides `legacy`, `revelations` and `reliable` modes. Direct `Belief` construction keeps `legacy` as its ablation default; the candidate `Agent` wires `belief_mode="reliable"` through the isolated worker. `revelations` preserves direct facts while disabling inferred item rules and soft replay. ## Stage 3: optional leads from actual moves and stats

**Problem and change.** The accepted `decision/lead.py` scorer prices type matchups only. A Pokémon's types do not tell us whether its supplied moves can threaten the opening opponent, whether it moves first, or whether it can set hazards, pivot or use Taunt safely. The optional `decision/lead_model.py` estimates these components using our exact request moves, stats, item and ability, and only opposing public preview/revelations plus compatible current set priors. It loads no enemy team export or private team file.

The explicit development weights are offense `1.0`, incoming damage `-0.55`, speed/priority tempo `0.12`, opening fragility `-0.30`, hazard utility `0.08`, pivot utility `0.06` and Taunt utility `0.05`. They were fixed without screening/confirmation outcome tuning. Opposing preview species receive equal lead mass; their compatible prior weights are normalised separately. Equivalent hypotheses differing only in unused Tera are merged by summing probability, without truncating that mass. Missing coverage uses a neutral revelation-preserving fallback. Ties are deterministic, and traces retain component scores, compatible-candidate counts, assumptions, fallback and chosen slot.

This is an approximate opening score, not a native first-turn rollout. It models selected damage/speed/priority/item/ability effects but omits weather, terrain, secondary effects, future switches and preview Tera. Variable power and conditional priority use explicit estimates. The unchanged type scorer remains an ablation.
